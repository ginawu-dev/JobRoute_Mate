Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

JobRoute Mate helps mobile service professionals schedule jobs, organize client information, keep service records, and review daily routes. This Privacy Policy explains the information that may be entered, stored, synchronized, or exported when using the app.

The current App Store privacy disclosure for JobRoute Mate states: Data Not Collected. This website and repository do not include an app implementation, so this policy is written conservatively from the public App Store listing and the public website content available in this repository.

Information stored by the app

JobRoute Mate allows users to enter and store information related to their work, including:

  • Client names and contact details
  • Client addresses and multiple saved addresses
  • Client phone numbers and additional contact records
  • Job details such as service date, start time, duration, address, price, and service notes
  • Payment status, reminders, and staff assignments
  • Service history, historical prices, service summaries, and service dates
  • Calendar entries and daily work list information

This information is used to help a professional keep track of jobs, clients, appointments, and daily routes. The app stores this information on the device and may make it available across supported Apple devices if iCloud synchronization is enabled.

Client and job information

JobRoute Mate supports adding new clients and selecting existing clients when creating a job. It can save multiple addresses and phone numbers for clients and can keep a record of client job history, service summaries, historical dates, and previous prices. Job-related records may also include service notes, payment status, and reminders.

We do not assume the developer receives these records unless there is clear evidence showing developer-side collection or a separate server or API. The available public information indicates that the app’s privacy disclosure is “Data Not Collected.”

Staff information

The app supports staff assignment and filtering of calendar and route information by staff member. Staff records, if entered into the app, are used to organise assignments and help view jobs by staff member. This information is limited to what the user enters and stores locally or synchronizes via iCloud.

Maps and route information

JobRoute Mate can display daily jobs on a map and provide estimated driving time and estimated driving distance. The App Store listing also states that route warnings can appear when service duration, travel time, and buffer time may create appointment conflicts. This does not imply continuous background GPS tracking or developer-operated route optimization services. The available public information does not show a requirement for constant location tracking.

Any location-related processing is limited to what the app supports for route planning on the user’s device and for route display based on job addresses and user-selected route information.

iCloud sync

JobRoute Mate supports synchronization of client, staff, and job information through Apple’s iCloud infrastructure. If enabled, this allows selected data to be available across supported Apple devices using the same Apple Account. The app is not described as operating a separate developer-owned cloud database for this data.

CSV export

Job and client information can be exported as CSV files for use in Excel or Numbers. These exported files are under the user’s control and may be shared through other apps, email, or cloud storage services chosen by the user.

Notifications

If reminders are enabled, the app may schedule notifications on the user’s device. Notification settings can be managed in the device settings, and app reminders are intended to support scheduling and service follow-up.

Third-party services

The public App Store privacy disclosure for JobRoute Mate states that data is not collected. The repository available for this project contains only website files and no app implementation, so there is no code-level evidence of analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, crash reporting services, developer-operated APIs, or third-party mapping services within the app source. This policy therefore does not claim any additional developer or third-party collection beyond the public App Store disclosure.

Data retention and deletion

Information in the app is retained based on the user’s records and usage of the app. User-controlled data can be deleted from the app, and synchronized data may also be managed through Apple device and iCloud settings. The public information available here does not provide a separate developer-managed deletion process beyond the user’s local app and iCloud controls.

Children’s privacy

JobRoute Mate is a business and professional scheduling app intended for service professionals and is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to this privacy policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
