Job scheduling built for mobile service work

Keep service appointments, client details, staff assignments, and daily routes in one place.

📅

Job scheduling

Add jobs with a service date, start time, job duration, address, price, service summary, payment status, notes, reminders, and staff assignment.

👥

Client management

Create new clients or choose existing ones, save multiple addresses and phone numbers, call clients from the app, and keep a record of job history, prices, service summaries, and dates.

🗺️

Route planning

Review daily jobs on a map, see estimated driving time and distance, filter routes by staff member, and get warnings when service duration, travel time, and buffer time may create appointment conflicts.

📊

Calendar & daily work

Use the calendar view to review schedules and daily work lists, see total job value, and filter calendar views by staff member.

👷

Staff-aware scheduling

Assign jobs to different staff members and filter route and calendar information by the person handling each job.

☁️

iCloud & export

Keep client, staff, and job information synchronized through iCloud and export job and client information as CSV files for Excel or Numbers.

Purpose-built for service professionals

JobRoute Mate is designed for handymen, cleaners, electricians, plumbers, gardeners, mobile technicians, contractors, and other professionals who travel between customer appointments. It helps you keep client details, future jobs, and daily route planning in one easy-to-review workflow.

What the app keeps organized

  • ✓Client information, addresses, and phone numbers
  • ✓Job dates, times, duration, pricing, notes, and payments
  • ✓Staff assignment and staff-based filters
  • ✓Daily routes, estimated driving time, and route warnings
  • ✓Service history for previous bookings and client activity

Who JobRoute Mate is for

It is built for professionals who organise jobs, keep client records, and move between appointments throughout the day.

Tradespeople

Handymen, electricians, plumbers, contractors, and other professionals scheduling onsite work.

Service teams

Small service teams who need shared scheduling visibility and staff filters without extra complexity.

Mobile specialists

Cleaners, gardeners, and technicians managing appointments, travel, and client follow-ups.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app for scheduling jobs and planning routes?

Yes. JobRoute Mate lets you schedule jobs and review daily routes on a map with estimated driving time and distance.

Can I manage clients and jobs on my iPhone?

Yes. The app is designed for iPhone and iPad and supports client records, job details, service history, reminders, and staff-aware scheduling.

Can JobRoute Mate show my daily jobs on a map?

Yes. Daily jobs can be reviewed on a map as part of the daily route view.

Can I see estimated travel time between jobs?

Yes. The app includes estimated driving time and estimated driving distance for daily routes.

Can I assign jobs to different staff members?

Yes. Jobs can be assigned to staff and route and calendar information can be filtered by staff member.

Can JobRoute Mate warn me about scheduling conflicts?

Yes. The app can provide route warnings when service duration, travel time, and buffer time may create appointment conflicts.

Can I keep a client's previous job history?

Yes. JobRoute Mate can show client job history, recorded prices, service summaries, and dates.

Can I export my jobs to Excel?

Yes. Job and client information can be exported as CSV files for Excel or Numbers.

Does JobRoute Mate sync with iCloud?

Yes. Client, staff, and job information can synchronize through iCloud.

Is JobRoute Mate suitable for cleaners, electricians, plumbers, and other mobile service professionals?

Yes. The App Store listing specifically identifies handymen, cleaners, electricians, plumbers, gardeners, mobile technicians, contractors, and other professionals who travel between customer appointments.
